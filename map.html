5 Primary Tourist Spots

Each spot below corresponds to one marker on the map above.

1. Liloan Baywalk

Sits right along the Poblacion shoreline, next to the AH26 coastal highway — the natural gathering spot along the water, a short walk from the town center.

View on Google Maps

Barangay Poblacion, Liloan, Southern Leyte 6612. (There's no separate street address for the baywalk itself — this is the barangay and ZIP code for that stretch of shoreline, based on its mapped coordinates.)

2. Molopolo Beach Resort

Up the coast near Barangay Molopolo, on the Himayangan boundary — a short drive north along the coastal road from Poblacion.

View on Google Maps

Barangay Molopolo, Liloan, Southern Leyte 6612, based on its mapped coordinates.

3. Fatima Hills

Rises just behind Poblacion, close enough to the town center to reach on foot or a short tricycle ride.

View on Google Maps

Barangay Fatima, Liloan, Southern Leyte 6612, based on its mapped coordinates. (Google Maps lists this pin as "Fatima Mountain Ridge.")

4. Tagbak Marine Park

A protected stretch of water off the Barangay Tagbak coastline, between the town center and Molopolo — home to seagrass beds that quietly pull CO2 out of the water.

View on Google Maps

In the coastal waters off Barangay Poblacion, Liloan, Southern Leyte 6612, based on its mapped coordinates.

5. Punta Rossette Park

Tucked into the coastline near Barangay Pres. Quezon, on the eastern side of town facing Dinagat Island.

View on Google Maps

Barangay Catig, Liloan, Southern Leyte 6612, based on its mapped coordinates.
